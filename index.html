This Privacy Policy describes how Core Nova Systems handles information in connection with the Block Blitz mobile game. By using Block Blitz, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

✅ Block Blitz does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The App works entirely offline on your device.

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your best score (stored in device SharedPreferences)
  • Sound on/off preference
  • Vibration on/off preference
  • Animations on/off preference
  • Current level progress

None of this data is transmitted to our servers or any third party. It is deleted when you uninstall the App.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Name, email address, or any personal identifiers
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, advertising ID, etc.)
  • Location data
  • Camera or microphone data
  • Contacts or calendar data
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to us
  • Cookies or tracking technologies

3. Internet Access

Block Blitz does not require or request internet access. The App runs completely offline. No data is sent to or received from any server.

4. Third-Party Libraries

  • AndroidX / Material Components (Google) — standard UI components, no data collection

5. Children's Privacy

Block Blitz is suitable for all ages, including children under 13. Because we do not collect any personal information, the App complies with COPPA and Google Play's Families Policy.

6. Data Security

Since Block Blitz stores all data locally and transmits nothing externally, your information is protected by your device's own security mechanisms.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

All locally stored data is automatically deleted when you uninstall the App. You can also reset your best score from within the App's Settings screen at any time.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
