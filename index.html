Gameplay, Scoring & Levels

T-03

Block Blitz is an 8×8 block puzzle game. You drag blocks from a tray of up to 5 pieces onto the board. Completing full rows or columns clears them and earns points. The game ends when no remaining block from the tray can be placed on the board.

Scoring System:

ActionPoints Earned
Place a block on the board+0 base
Clear 1 line (row or column)+100
Clear 2 lines simultaneously+250
Clear 3 lines simultaneously+400
Clear 4 or more lines simultaneously+600
Combo bonus (per combo chain)+50

Level Progression: The game features 10 levels. Score multipliers increase as you advance, rewarding strategic play. Your best score and current level are saved locally on your device.

ℹ️ Scores are stored only on your device. There is no online leaderboard — your score cannot be submitted or compared externally.
