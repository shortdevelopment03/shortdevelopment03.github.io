01

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Core Nova Systems ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the Block Blitz mobile application ("App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices.

Short version: Block Blitz does not collect any personal information. The App is fully offline. All game data stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

02

Information We Collect

Block Blitz does NOT collect any personal information. We do not collect your name, email, address, phone number, device identifiers, IP address, location, or any other personal data.

The only data stored is local game data on your device via Android SharedPreferences:

  • Best score and last level reached
  • Sound, vibration, and animation preferences
  • Language preference

This data never leaves your device and is never accessible to us.

03

How We Use Data

The locally stored data is used solely for the following purposes — entirely on your device:

  • Display your personal best score and progress within the game
  • Remember your gameplay preferences across sessions
  • Ensure smooth gameplay without requiring account creation

No data is processed, analyzed, or used for any other purpose by us.

04

Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do NOT share any information with third parties — because we do not collect any information in the first place. Block Blitz:

  • Does NOT require internet connection to play
  • Does NOT have analytics or tracking
  • Does NOT have advertisements
  • Does NOT require user registration or login
  • Does NOT access your contacts, photos, or other personal files
  • Does NOT use third-party SDKs that collect data
05

Android Permissions

Block Blitz requests only the following Android permission:

Permission Purpose Required?
VIBRATE Haptic feedback when placing blocks and clearing lines Optional — can be disabled in settings

Note: Block Blitz does NOT request or use any other permissions — no camera, location, storage, contacts, microphone, or network access.

06

Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Your game data is protected by your device's built-in security features
  • We do not have access to your device data
  • There is no risk of data breaches from our servers (because we don't have servers)
  • Your data remains private and entirely under your control

We recommend keeping your device secure with a lock screen PIN and keeping your Android OS updated for optimal security.

07

Children's Privacy

Block Blitz is suitable for all ages, including children. Since we do not collect any personal information, our app is fully compliant with COPPA and similar regulations worldwide.

  • We do NOT collect personal information from children
  • Children are not required to provide any information to play
  • We do NOT share children's information with third parties
  • No advertisements or in-app purchases are present
08

Third-Party Services

Block Blitz does NOT integrate with any third-party services, including:

  • Analytics (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, etc.)
  • Advertising networks (AdMob, Facebook Audience Network, etc.)
  • Social media platforms (Facebook, Twitter, etc.)
  • Cloud storage or crash reporting services

The app operates completely independently and offline.

09

International Data Transfers

Since Block Blitz does not collect or transmit any data, there are no international data transfers. All your game data stays on your device regardless of your location.

10

Your Rights & Choices

You have complete control over your data:

10.1 Access Your Data

Your game data (scores, settings) is stored locally on your device and can be viewed within the app interface.

10.2 Delete Your Data

You can delete all game data by uninstalling the app, or by going to Settings → Apps → Block Blitz → Clear Data.

10.3 Opt-Out Options

Optional features you can disable at any time in-game: sound effects, vibration feedback, and animations.

11

Game Features & Functionality

All features operate locally on your device:

  • Sound Effects: Local audio files (MP3/WAV) — no external libraries
  • Haptic Feedback: Optional vibration for enhanced gameplay
  • Animations: Android's native animation framework — no data-collecting libraries
  • Score Tracking: Local storage of best score and current level
  • Multi-language: Language preference stored locally on device
