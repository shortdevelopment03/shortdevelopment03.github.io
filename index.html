Privacy Policy
Last Updated: April 07, 2026
TL;DR: Block Blitz collects zero personal data. No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no permissions. Your best score is stored only on your device. This is the most privacy-respecting puzzle game you'll ever play.
01
Overview

Block Blitz is a classic block puzzle game developed by Core Nova System. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices in the simplest terms possible: we collect nothing.

This policy applies to the Block Blitz Android application (package name com.choco.blockblitz).

02
Local Data Storage

The only data Block Blitz stores locally on your device:

  • ✓
    Your best score — Saved in SharedPreferences to track your progress
  • ✓
    Sound preference — Whether you have sound effects enabled or disabled

This data is stored in app-private storage and is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app. It never leaves your device.

03
What We Don't Collect

Block Blitz does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

  • ✗
    No personal information — No name, email, phone, or contact data
  • ✗
    No location data — The app does not access GPS or network location
  • ✗
    No device identifiers — No IMEI, advertising ID, or fingerprinting
  • ✗
    No analytics — No third-party analytics SDK is integrated
  • ✗
    No advertising data — Block Blitz is completely ad-free
04
App Permissions

Block Blitz requests zero Android permissions. The AndroidManifest.xml contains no permission declarations.

  • ✗
    No INTERNET permission — Cannot make network requests
  • ✗
    No camera or microphone access
  • ✗
    No storage permissions
  • ✗
    No contacts or calendar access
  • ✗
    No phone, SMS, or call access
  • ✗
    No Bluetooth or Wi-Fi access

You can verify this yourself in Settings → Apps → Block Blitz → Permissions.

05
Sound Effects

All sound effects are sourced from Pixabay (pixabay.com) under the Pixabay Content License, which permits free use.

Block Place Line Clear Level Up Game Over Button Tap Combo Sounds

Sound effects are bundled in the app and played locally using Android's SoundPool API. No network activity is involved.

06
Third-Party Libraries

Block Blitz uses only standard Android libraries:

AndroidX AppCompat Material Components ConstraintLayout Lottie (animations)

None of these libraries collect personal data or make network requests. Lottie is used purely for local UI animations. No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are included.

07
Children's Privacy

Block Blitz is suitable for all ages. Since the app collects no personal information whatsoever and requires no account creation, it is completely safe for children to use.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

08
Data Security

Your best score and sound preference are stored in Android's SharedPreferences. This data:

  • ✓
    Is stored only on your device in private storage
  • ✓
    Is automatically deleted when you uninstall
  • ✓
    Is never transmitted to any server
  • ✓
    Cannot be accessed by other apps

Because no data leaves your device, traditional data breach scenarios do not apply.

09
Google Play Store

Block Blitz is distributed via the Google Play Store. Google Play may collect data about downloads and usage as part of its platform operations, governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Our Play Store data safety declaration:

  • ✓
    No data collected from users
  • ✓
    No data shared with third parties
  • ✓
    No encryption required (no data transmitted)
10
Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top.

Since the app collects no personal data, changes are unlikely to affect your privacy. Significant changes will be noted in the Play Store update changelog.

11
User Rights

Since Block Blitz does not collect any personal data, most data rights are inherently satisfied. However, you retain the following rights:

  • ✓
    Right to deletion — Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all locally stored data (best score, sound preference)
  • ✓
    Right to access — You can view your stored data by checking your device's app storage settings
  • ✓
    Right to opt-out — There is nothing to opt out of, as no tracking or data collection occurs

For any privacy-related questions or concerns, please contact us at the email below.

12
Governing Law & Compliance

This Privacy Policy is intended to comply with applicable privacy laws, including:

GDPR (EU) CCPA (California) COPPA (Children) Google Play Policy

As Block Blitz collects no personal data, it operates fully within these regulations by design. If you are a resident of a jurisdiction with specific privacy rights and have questions, feel free to contact us.
