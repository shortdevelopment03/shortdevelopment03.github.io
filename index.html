01
Overview

Block Blitz Choco is a puzzle game developed by Core Nova System. This Privacy Policy explains what information is stored by the app, what information may be processed by third-party advertising services, and what choices you have as a user.

This policy applies to the Android application Block Blitz Choco (package name com.choco.blockblitz).

Important: We do not operate our own analytics or user-account system inside the app. However, the app includes Google AdMob rewarded ads, so certain data may be processed by Google to deliver ads and rewards.

02
Local Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores a limited amount of information locally on your device to support gameplay and settings:

  • ✓
    Best score - Saved locally so your progress can be shown inside the game
  • ✓
    Sound preference - Whether sound effects are enabled or disabled

This information is stored in app-private local storage on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the app's data.

03
Rewarded Ads and Extra Life Feature

Block Blitz Choco includes rewarded ads provided by Google AdMob. These ads may be shown when you voluntarily choose to watch an ad in exchange for an in-game benefit, such as an extra life or similar reward.

  • ✓
    Rewarded ads are optional - You choose whether to watch an ad for a reward
  • ✓
    No forced account creation - You do not need to create an account to play
  • ✓
    Ads are served by Google - Not by our own server infrastructure

Because rewarded ads are delivered by Google AdMob, Google may process certain information to make ad delivery work, prevent abuse, measure performance, and determine reward completion.

04
Information Google AdMob May Process

When rewarded ads are requested or displayed, Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or process certain data. Depending on device, configuration, region, and Google settings, this can include:

  • ✓
    Advertising identifiers - Such as the Android Advertising ID where available
  • ✓
    IP address and approximate location - Often used for fraud prevention, ad delivery, and regional compliance
  • ✓
    Device and app information - Such as device model, OS version, language, app version, and network details
  • ✓
    Ad interaction data - Such as ad requests, impressions, taps, reward completion events, and basic performance diagnostics
  • ✓
    Diagnostics and fraud prevention signals - Used to maintain ad quality, security, and billing integrity

We do not directly receive all of this information in raw form. Much of it is processed by Google under its own privacy practices.

For more information, please review:

Google Privacy Policy Google Mobile Ads Data Disclosure AdMob Rewarded Ads

  • ✗
